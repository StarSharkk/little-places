Little Places

The house rules behind ten little shops — human-scale proportions, a warm palette, ink lines, white paving and a soft blue afternoon shadow — written down so Claude can build the eleventh. Name a shop and you get a small Three.js building with a door to open, a room inside and a few things to play with.

mkdir -p ~/.claude/skills && curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/StarSharkk/little-places/main/skill/little-places-building.skill -o /tmp/little-places.skill && unzip -oq /tmp/little-places.skill -d ~/.claude/skills

For Claude Code, run this once in a terminal. On Claude.ai, download the zip instead and add it to your Skills in settings.
